About

This is a community-run compatibility database for this fork of touchHLE, an emulator for early iPhone OS apps.

It tracks which apps work in touchHLE, at what rating, on which platforms and with which touchHLE versions. Anyone can submit a compatibility report for an app they've tested.

Inspiration

This site is modelled on the original appdb.touchhle.org by the upstream touchHLE project. It is an independent community fork, not affiliated with upstream.

House rules

  • Only test apps you legally own.
  • No links to pirated content. “Abandonware” is still piracy.
  • Be precise — include the touchHLE version, your OS, your GPU.
